Oil Markets and Prices/U.S. Military Operations in Iran

Q. On Iran, oil prices have shot back up over the last 24 hours since we last talked.

The President. No, the prices will drop very—very rapidly as soon as we win the military conflict or war—you can call it whatever you want. The prices will go back to where they were.

It's a—it's always—look, there's no cost-cutter like me, but Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon. When that ends—and I think it will end very shortly after the election; it could end before the election, but I think it will end very shortly after the election—the prices will go down to less than $2 a barrel—$2 a gallon for oil.

Q. How can you be sure of that, sir?

The President. For gasoline, I'm pretty sure. I'm pretty good at it. I've been—if you look at where it was before I started—and I had to start. And if I had to do it again, I'd do it exactly the same. And they'll never have a nuclear weapon. We can never allow them to have a nuclear weapon.

So, before I started—literally, the day before—I was in Iowa. And I saw it at $1.85 and

$1.95 for gasoline—a gallon. And I think it's going to match those numbers—maybe even be, at some points, below those numbers.

[At this point, several reporters began asking questions at once.]

But the big thing—bigger than that, more important than that: They won't have a nuclear weapon.

Late Turning Point U.S.A. Founder Charles J. Kirk

Q. Mr. President, on the 1-year anniversary of Charlie Kirk's assassination, what, in your view, is his legacy? And can you speak to the impact he had on millions of young Americans?

The President. Yes, what Charlie did was incredible, with young Americans more than anybody else. I mean, I know him so well, and he was always such a great supporter. And it's so sad. I mean, you think about it. It's just a sad cloud over the country, what happened to Charlie. It's not even believable.

He was very close to me and my family, but he was great. I think he's got—there's great memories of Charlie.

But more than anything else, what he was able to do with young people. He just—young people loved him. They still do.

Artificial Intelligence/China

Q. Do you have any concerns about AI leading to human extinction?

The President. No, I don't have any. I have concerns that if we don't win AI, we're going to be put in a very bad position.

We are leading China right now by a pretty good period. I would say a year, which is, you know, considered a lot. President Xi is coming over very shortly. He's going to be—we're going to—we're having a state dinner. It's going to be—I think it's going to be great.

No, we're leading China. We're leading the world right now at everything. We have the greatest economy in history, and people are going to start to see it. We have more jobs right now than at any time in the history of our country.
