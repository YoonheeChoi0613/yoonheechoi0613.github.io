Steve has a wonderful way about him and people like him. And even in this case, both sides like him, and he was able to make a deal. That deal would have never been made without Steve.

The Biden people couldn't make the deal. They were working on it for a year and a half.

They couldn't make a deal. We got it done prior to the Inauguration. We said it has to be before the Inauguration.

I mean, the deal should hold, but if it doesn't hold, there'll be a lot of problems. [Several reporters spoke at once.]
